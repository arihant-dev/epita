## Communication Management

## Communication management Process
1. Stakeholder identification
2. Communication planification
3. Distribute information
4. Managing Stakeholder expectations
5. Report performance

- The process required to ensure the timely production, collection, distribution, storage, retrieval, and ultimate disposition of project information.

# Few aspects of communication
- Internal - External
- Formal - Informal
- Vertical - Horizontal
- Written - Oral
- Official - Unofficial
- Verbal - Non-verbal

# Number of communication channels
- n(n-1)/2
- n = number of stakeholders

# Communication Methods
- Interactive communication - multi direction (meetings, video conferencing)
- Push communication - one direction (emails, memos)
- Pull communication - access when needed (intranet sites, knowledge repositories)

## The 5 W's of communication
- What - information to be communicated
- What - needs to be communicated
- What - is the format
- When - frequency of communication
- Who - is responsible for communication

- Status reports
- Progress reports

## Risk Management

# Risk Management Process
1. Risk Management planning
2. Risk identification
3. Qualitative risk analysis
4. Quantitative risk analysis
5. Risk mitigation planning
6. Control risks  

- Risk is an uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, affects at least one project objective
- Risk Managemtn Objectives:
    - Increase the probability and impact of positive events(opportunities)
    - Decrease the probability and impact of negative events(threats)

# Exogenous Risks
- Political
- Meteorological
- Social
- Regulatory

# Endogenous Risks
- Technological
- Operational
- Financial
- Commercial

## Purchasing Management Process
1. Plan supplies and contracts
2. Conduct procurements
3. Control procurements
- Procurement Management includes the processes necessary to purchase or acquire products, services, or results needed from outside the project team.



# Request for Proposal (RFP)
- A document that solicits proposal, often made through a bidding process, by an agency or company interested in procurement of a commodity, service, or valuable asset, to potential suppliers to submit business proposals.


# Stakeholder Management

## Stakeholder Management Process
1. Identify stakeholders
2. Plan the management of stakeholders
3. Manage stakeholder commitments
4. Master stakeholder commitments

- Regular Communication
- Structured Meetings

## Stakeholder Influence Matrix - Garner
- High Influence, High Interest - Manage Closely
- High Influence, Low Interest - Keep Satisfied
- Low Influence, High Interest - Keep Informed
- Low Influence, Low Interest - Monitor
