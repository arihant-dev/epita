## Project Management Principles - Lecture 7 Notes

## Conflict Management
- Conflicts can be beneficial(one of the opportunities for growth) or detrimental(harmful to project progress).


## Different types of management are needed at different stages of project:

| Project Stage        | Conflict Management Style     | Type 
|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Early Stage          | Avoiding                      |
| Planning Stage       | Collaborating                 |
| Execution Stage      | Compromising                  |
| Closing Stage        | Collaborating / Accommodating |
