## Introduction


### Inbound Marketing

- The inbound marketing is best described  as passive sales model. Unlike outbound marketing, inbound marketing focuses on attracting customers through content and interactions that are relevant and helpful, rather than interruptive. Inbound marketing earns the attention of customers, makes the company easy to be found, and draws customers to the website by producing interesting content.

### Omnichannel Marketing

- Omnichannel is a term used on ecommerce and retail to describe a business strategy that aims to provide a seameless shopping experience for customers across all channels, whether they are shopping online from a mobile device, a laptop, or in a brick-and-mortar store. The goal of omnichannel marketing is to create a consistent and integrated customer experience across all touchpoints, allowing customers to interact with the brand in a way that is convenient and personalized to their needs.
