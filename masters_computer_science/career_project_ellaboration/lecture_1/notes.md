## Soft Skills

- Identify the soft-skill
- Relate it to a experience
- Create a story around it

    -[] Circumstances - Where?, Who?, When?
    -[] Mission
    -[] Analysis of the initial situation
    -[] Actions - What did you do?
    -[] Results - What was the outcome? with metrics
    -[] Learnings - What did you learn from the experience? How did it help you grow personally and professionally?

Example:
Problem Solving -  Designed Kafka-based pipelines processing 50k+ events/s to power near real-time analytics for banking partners.

In my last company, I was responsible for designing and implementing the feature that allowed our banking partners to recieve near real-time analytics on their user's transactions. The challenge was to process a high volume of events, around 50k+ evetns per second, while ensuring low latency and high reliability. To achieve this, I designed a Kafka-based pipeline that could handle the high throughout and provide real-time analytics to our partners. The result was significant improvement in the performance and reliability of our analytics systems. This experience taught me the how to design and implement scalable data processing pipelines in a high-pressure environment.

Example:
Golang - Distributed migration: 
In my last company, we needed to migrate a legacy PHP monolith to a Go microservices architecture. The challenge was to ensure a smooth transition while minimizing downtime and maintaining the integrity of our services. I coordinated the migration process, working closely with cross-functional teams to plan and execute the transition. This involved breaking down the monolith into smaller, manageable microservices, implementing new features in Go, and ensuring that all services were properly integrated. The result was a significant reduction in technical debt and a 30% improvement in critical API response times. This experience taught me how to manage complex migrations and work effectively with cross-functional teams to achieve successful outcomes.
