# Lecture 3 Notes

## Profile Headline
 
- LinkedIn Headline
- Github Bio Headline
- Personal Website Headline

> The headline should make you stand out and be memorable.
> Can use the fact that you are a international student to your advantage, as it can be a unique selling point.

## Profile Summary - About Me

> Catch the reader's attention and make them want to read more.
> Stick to 3-5 short paragraphs, and use bullet points to make it easy to read.
> Use Keywords

### Tips

- 800 to 1200 characters
- Use Keywords
- Use bold and italics to highlight important information
- Use general interest to make it more engaging and relatable to a wider audience
