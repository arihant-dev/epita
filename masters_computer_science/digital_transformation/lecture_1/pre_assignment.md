## Answer

## Topic: Blockchain

**Position:** Early Adopters

**Why:** While Bitcoin has high awareness, Blockchain as a broader technology (smart contracts, supply chain) has not yet achieved a "Dominant Design". Many enterprises are running pilots and PoCs, but few have moved to full production. Regulatory uncertainty and lack of standards hinder wider adoption.

**To move Innovators → Early Adopters:**

- To move to Early Adopters, the technology must solve a specific, high-value problem
- Show measurable ROI from pilots

**To move Early Majority → Late Majority:**

- A single "Dominant Design" (standard interface/protocol) must win the marketplace. The Late Majority will not adopt if there are competing, incompatible standards.
- The Network Effects must be so strong that it becomes a disadvantage not to use the technology.
